Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Calendula app for iPhone (“the app”) handles personal data. The app is provided by Pavel Kutov, an individual developer (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller. Contact: kut.contact.it@gmail.com.

Your calendar and task content

Events, tasks, notes and settings you create are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your own iCloud account and processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not access this content.

Device permissions

If you allow it, the app accesses your Calendar, Reminders and Contacts (birthdays and anniversaries) and uses notifications and alarms, only to provide its features on your device. You can withdraw permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Data we may collect

  • Diagnostics. Crash reports, performance data (such as freezes and slow screens) and technical information (device model, iOS version, app version, language, an anonymous installation identifier) to find and fix problems. This data is not used to identify you or to advertise.
  • Account data. If account features are available and you choose to create an account, your email address and an account identifier, used to provide the account and its features and to contact you about it.
  • Messages to support. If you email us, your email address and message, used to reply.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

Legal bases (EU/EEA/UK)

We process diagnostics and support messages on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and account data to perform our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b)). Where the law requires consent, we ask for it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Service providers

We may use trusted service providers, such as crash reporting, analytics or hosting services, that process data on our behalf and under our instructions. They may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data or use it for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. Diagnostics are deleted or anonymized once no longer needed to fix problems. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including the EU, EEA, UK and California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. If you have an account, you can delete it in the app or by contacting us. To make a request, email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version is always on this page, with the date above. If changes are significant, we will let you know in the app.
